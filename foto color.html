EXPERIMENTAL & COLOR

↓ Haz scroll hacia abajo para interactuar

This body of work investigates the expressive possibilities of the analog medium through non-traditional photographic methodologies, chemigrams, solarization (the Sabattier effect), and direct chemical intervention on light-sensitive emulsion. Through crystalline reactions, manual exposures, and reactive developer stains, the photographic surface transcends representation to become a tactile topography where organic and aquatic motifs emerge directly from chemical dialogue.

Intersecting with experimental practice, the urban landscape of Bogotá unfolds through sharp chromatic contrasts and pinhole captures. From the frenetic flight of pigeons scattered across sun-drenched cobblestones in historic public plazas to stark architectural colonnades punctuated by vivid industrial signals, each composition investigates how light carves space, isolates color accents, and reimagines everyday architectural transit into moments of suspended geometry.

The series culminates in contemplative encounters between human presence and atmospheric twilight. Nocturnal horizons capture red-brick skylines receding beneath mountain mists streaked with electric movement, while intimate darkroom studies explore the human form through deep chiaroscuro and delicate tonal transitions. Across every piece, photography operates not merely as documentation, but as an experimental alchemy of light, chemistry, and space.
