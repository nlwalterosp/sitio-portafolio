BIO

I am a multi-disciplinary Digital Artist with over three years of experience, bridging the gap between imagination and interactive reality. My core expertise lies in working as a 3D Generalist and Concept Artist, where I specialize in creating highly optimized, stylized assets for real-time applications.

My practice extends beyond simple asset creation into the realms of illustration and visual design. I am deeply passionate about illustrative storytelling and focus on creating cohesive visual worlds, from initial concept art to final implementation.

Possessing an advanced, end-to-end understanding of full production pipelines, I am skilled in comprehensive modeling (sculpting and retopology), PBR texturing, and integrating complex assets into the Unity engine. As an interactive artist, I design and build immersive experiences, particularly within Augmented Reality (AR) and Extended Reality (XR) platforms.

I am driven by a passion for crafting engaging visual content for games and diverse digital media, maintaining a fierce commitment to balancing high visual quality with optimized, high-performance execution.
