BIO

3D Generalist and Concept Artist with 3+ years of experience creating stylized assets for real-time applications, games, XR, and interactive media. Skilled in 3D modeling (hard surface & organic), retopology, UV mapping, PBR texturing, and integrating game-ready assets into Unity for interactive and Augmented Reality (AR) experiences.

Experienced in full production pipelines from concept to final implementation, with a strong focus on optimization, visual quality, and real-time performance. Background in Fine Arts from Universidad Nacional de Colombia combining deep plastic and sculptural sensibilities with modern real-time rendering, lighting, and animation.

I work in teams with an organized, flexible, and creative mindset and an innovative vision; dynamic, proactive, and committed to facing and advancing through challenging creative problems.
