DESIGN AND ART

↓ Haz scroll hacia abajo para interactuar

Plastic and sculptural research focusing on the intersection of light, matter, and contemporary organic spaces. Through a deep exploration of form and void, these artworks challenge our perception of physical boundaries.

This ongoing series investigates subtle light variations, the ephemeral nature of plant life cycles, and the intricate textures that inhabit the penumbra. It invites the viewer to reflect on the silent dialogue between shadow and illumination.

Each piece is meticulously developed through a combination of mixed media techniques, site-specific installations, and the detailed photographic recording of natural transformation processes over time, capturing the delicate decay and rebirth of organic materials.
