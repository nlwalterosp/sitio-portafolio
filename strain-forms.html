STRAIN FORMS

This interactive project explores the balanced, self-regulating energy of the toroid—nature's primary model for life at any scale.

By clicking the interface, users deform and reconfigure an experimental drawing on exposed photographic paper, dynamically recreating these geometric forms.

The work visualizes toroidal movement across different scales, connecting the macrocosm of Earth's electromagnetic field with the microcosm of human energy centers, bridging our internal and external worlds.
