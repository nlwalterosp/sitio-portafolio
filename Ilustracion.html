TRADITIONAL ILLUSTRATION

↓ Haz scroll hacia abajo para interactuar

This collection explores the human countenance and psychological interiority through expressive watercolor and ink portraiture. Utilizing wet-on-wet washes, raw pigment bleeding, and spontaneous gestural linework, each portrait captures fleeting emotional states—from the mischievous, freckled innocence of childhood to the weathered dignity and contemplative vulnerability of old age. Color and water operate organically across the paper, allowing human character to emerge between definition and dissolution.

Drawing inspiration from traditional sumi-e ink washes and biological observation, a series of aquatic animal studies examines the fluid anatomy and dynamic rhythm of marine life. Rapid brushstrokes and delicate gradients delineate the slippery contours of fish, the delicate articulation of fins, and the tactile tension of aquatic movement, synthesizing technical anatomical precision with atmospheric oriental wash techniques.

A dedicated focus on wildlife illustration delves into the structural complexity of the animal kingdom through disciplined pen-and-ink drafting. Employing intricate cross-hatching, stippling, and layered linear densities, these drawings capture the visceral power of animal anatomy—from the raw, cavernous roar and textured dermis of a hippopotamus to the delicate, suspended equilibrium of primates and spiny contours of curled mammals in quiet repose.
