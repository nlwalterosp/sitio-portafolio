THE DREAM OF CAROTENOIDS

This installation creates an environment where sound fosters a relationship with Solenostemon (coleus) plants and garden flowers like pansies and petunias. These species are rich in carotenoids, photosynthetic pigments that produce vibrant red hues and adapt to varying lighting conditions.

The artwork is fully interactive, activated by any environmental noise, including the human voice. A custom-built system uses a microphone to read ambient sound frequencies, translating them into specific light frequencies that illuminate and nourish the plants.

Simultaneously, this auditory input triggers three animations illustrating the plants' biological processes. The installation only comes alive when there is sound; in silence, it remains in darkness.
